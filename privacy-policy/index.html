Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how Foreigner Studio collects and uses personal data when you visit foreignerstudio.com or get in touch with us. We process data in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Spain's Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD).

1. Who is responsible

Controller: Fábio Ribeiro de Almeida Souza, self-employed, trading as Foreigner Studio.
Based in: Valencia, Spain.
Email: hello@foreignerstudio.com

2. What data we collect

  • Contact form and email: your name, email address, company and the details of your project that you choose to send us.
  • Website usage: technical data such as IP address, device, browser and pages visited, collected through cookies and similar technologies (see our Cookie Policy).

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal: pre-contractual measures and our legitimate interest in answering you.
  • To deliver and invoice services you hire: performance of a contract and legal obligations (tax and accounting).
  • To understand how the website is used and keep it secure: legitimate interest and, for non-essential cookies, your consent.

4. How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept while we talk and for up to 24 months afterwards unless a project starts. Invoicing data is kept for the period required by Spanish tax and commercial law. Analytics data is kept for the period shown in the Cookie Policy.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We use trusted providers that process data on our behalf, such as Squarespace (website hosting, forms and analytics) and our email provider. Some of them may be located outside the European Economic Area; in that case transfers rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, rectify, delete, restrict or port your data, and to object to its processing. You can also withdraw consent whenever you wish. Write to hello@foreignerstudio.com. If you feel your rights have not been respected you may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
